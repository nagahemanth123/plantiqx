Website & online presence audit

What plantiqx.com and freightbid.plantiqx.com say to a plant head today, what gets in the way of new clients, and what to fix first.

Prepared by Busy Fox · 18 November 2026 · Checked live on desktop

In one minute

The site has the right promise and a strong product list, but it reads like a template, claims more than the company’s own profile, and hides the proof that would win a mid-size plant. Shared links show up bare on LinkedIn and WhatsApp, which matters because those are the channels the social plan depends on.

PositioningRight idea, weak proof

“One platform” is there. SETL, the founders, live clients and the furnace project are not.

Shareability & searchFix first

No meta description or link-preview tags. robots.txt and sitemap.xml serve the homepage.

ConsistencyMixed signals

Two spellings of the name, two email domains, product names that differ from the profile.

What already works

  • A clear tagline and logo

    “Where Minds and Machines Merge” and the wordmark (the slashed “a” echoes the X) give a recognisable identity to build on.

  • A demo button that’s always visible

    “Request For Demo” sits in the header and repeats through the page.

  • An FAQ that answers buyer questions

    Integration with ERP and SCADA, security, 1–4 week rollouts, multi-plant use. These are the questions a plant head asks.

  • Analytics and an assistant

    Google Analytics 4 is installed and an “IQX Assistant” chat greets visitors, so the plumbing for tracking leads exists.

  • FreightBid stands on its own

    Separate shipper and driver sign-ups, reverse-auction bidding, e-KYC, GPS tracking and proof of delivery are explained clearly.

  • Social links in the footer

    LinkedIn and Instagram (@plantiqx.ai) are linked, ready for the social plan to feed.

Copy: say this, not this

On the site today
Plant-floor version
PlantIQX is revolutionizing industrial operations with smart automation.
One login for your machines, gate, cameras, energy and freight.
AI-powered video intelligence that monitors your facility 24/7.
Cameras that flag a missing helmet or an after-hours entry while the shift is still on. Now piloting.
A 360° view into your operations.
See which machine stopped, where, and for how long, across every plant.
Integrates seamlessly with existing equipment.
Works with the machines you already have: Modbus, OPC-UA and MQTT, wired or LTE.

Quick wins this week

  • Add meta description and link-preview tags to every page
  • Remove the internal-note headings and the empty “Real Numbers” block
  • Tag every module Live, Piloting or Coming soon
  • Fix the email and pick one spelling: PlantiqX
  • Add “Backed by SETL” and the two founders
  • Add “by PlantiqX” to FreightBid
  • Serve real robots.txt and sitemap.xml files
  • Set up a YouTube channel for demo clips (none is linked today)

Not checked tonight: LinkedIn and Instagram follower counts and post history (both need a login), page-speed lab scores, and how the site renders on phones. We will cover these in the first-week review.
