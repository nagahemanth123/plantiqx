Logo

A purple wordmark with an orange-accented X, drawn as custom lettering. Always use the original artwork; never retype the name to make a logo.

Construction

Custom lettering, not a typeface. The “a” carries a diagonal slash that echoes the X, so the word reads as one engineered piece. In the X, the purple stroke (/) runs in front and the orange stroke (\) breaks around it. Shown left: the vector redraw of the X, for icons only.

09TypographyPlantiqX Brand Kit · v1 · November 2026

Voice and tone

PlantiqX sounds like an engineering company: precise, plain-spoken and practical. Write the way a good plant head talks on the floor.

01

Name the machine, not the magic

Say which furnace, which gate, which meter. Specific beats impressive.

02

Use case first, technology second

Start with the problem the plant has. Bring in IoT or AI only to explain how it is solved.

03

Plain words, plant units

Short sentences. The units and terms the plant already uses: °C, kWh, shifts, gate passes, downtime.

Say thisNot this
Know when the furnace crosses its set point.Unlock AI-powered operational excellence.
Every visitor, vehicle and gate pass, logged at the gate.Seamless, next-generation visitor experiences.
Fit sensors to the motors you already run.Revolutionise your legacy infrastructure.
See which DG set burned the extra diesel last night.Leverage real-time insights to drive sustainability.
Book a plant visit.Begin your digital transformation journey today!

Avoid: seamless, leverage, cutting-edge, next-gen, revolutionise, unlock, synergy, exclamation marks. Use: plant head, shift, set point, gate pass, LT panel, downtime, breakdown.

10Voice and tonePlantiqX Brand Kit · v1 · November 2026

Photography

Big industry, shot like cinema. Large working plants, real people in proper PPE, heavy machines and true site light, with scale and depth in every frame.

Do

  • Large plants and heavy machines: furnaces, process halls, panel rooms, gates, yards.
  • Cinematic framing: wide establishing shots, low angles, depth and scale.
  • People in the right PPE for the area: helmet, safety shoes, hi-vis, coverall.
  • True site light and colour. Brownfield machines as well as new.

Don't

  • Glowing circuits, holograms, sci-fi robots or floating dashboards.
  • Stock handshakes, posed office teams, people without PPE on the floor.
  • Purple or orange colour washes and heavy filters.

Direction images made by Busy Fox for this kit. Replace with photos shot at PlantiqX customer sites as they come in.

One diagonal stroke, taken from the X at 54°, crops photos and divides layouts. Status badges say what is running today.
