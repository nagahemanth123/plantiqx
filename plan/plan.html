Targets set by Busy Fox for planning, not forecasts or past results. They assume the cadence in section 05, both founders posting weekly and the Starter paid tier from Month 2. Reset against real baselines at day 30; monthly targets in section 11.

What we don’t promise: viral posts, follower spikes or leads in week one. Plant buyers move slowly. Month 1 builds the base that Months 2 and 3 convert.

Why a plant head will respond to this

Change feels riskyPlant heads protect running lines. “Brownfield first” and “nothing gets replaced” answer that fear before it is raised.
Honesty earns trustSaying “in testing” out loud makes “live” believable. Buyers who catch one overclaim stop reading.
Familiar names get the meetingB2B buyers need many touches before they reply. Two founders posting every week, plus retargeting, keeps the name in front of them.
Small steps, then bigger onesA poll, then a checklist, then a free audit, then a demo. Each step is easy, and each makes the next one more likely.

Content pillars and formats

Five kinds of post, in a fixed mix. Most of the weight goes on plant problems and proof, because that is what a plant head stops scrolling for.

Content pillarShareWhat goes in itMain formats
Plant problems, in plant words30%One problem per post: the gate register, the late breakdown, the power bill, the freight phone calls. The problem first, PlantiqX second.Carousels, before/after, polls

How it works20%Modbus, OPC-UA and MQTT retrofits, set value vs present value, single sign-on, visitor data under the DPDP Act.YouTube explainers, LinkedIn documents
Founder perspective20%Krishna Kanth on how plants really run. Srikanth on plant data. Opinions, lessons, the reasons behind the product.Text posts, talking clips
Company and people5%The team, the summit, SETL, hiring, milestones the client allows us to share.Photos, stories

Formats we’ll make

  • LinkedIn carousels. Six to eight slides, one idea, plant language, purple and orange templates.
  • Shop-floor clips. 20–45 seconds, real sound, English captions. Telugu or Hindi versions on Instagram where it helps.
  • Founder posts. 150–250 words, one real photo, no hashtag walls.
  • Screen demos. 60–90 seconds on dummy data. No client data ever appears on screen.
  • Before / after. Paper register vs VEMS. One bill vs per-machine energy. Phone calls vs bids.
  • YouTube explainers. Four to six minutes, cut into Shorts and LinkedIn clips.
  • Polls. One a month, to learn how plants work today and to build retargeting lists.

How we keep it coming

  • One founder interview a week, 30–45 minutes, recorded. Each one gives three or four posts.
  • One shoot day a month at a site where PlantiqX has written permission, plus the PlantiqX office and test bench.
  • A technical check by a PlantiqX engineer on anything that names a protocol, a reading or a module status.
  • Founders approve every post that goes out under their name. Nothing is published as them without a read.
  • A two-week content bank, so a busy week at the plant never means a silent week online.

What we offer in exchange

The Plant Data Audit (free). A PlantiqX engineer walks one area with the plant team: the gate, a machine line or the energy panel. The plant gets a one-page note on where its data sits today, what can be connected without replacing equipment, and the first step worth taking. In person in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh, on video elsewhere.

Brownfield Readiness Checklist (PDF). Built from the summit talk. For people not ready to talk yet; it starts the follow-up.

Where they sign up. One landing page on plantiqx.com with one short form (name, role, company, plant location, what’s hurting, WhatsApp opt-in). The same fields in a LinkedIn lead-gen form, so nobody has to leave LinkedIn.

Follow-up, by WhatsApp and email

  1. Day 0Thank you, the checklist or booking link, and who will call
  2. Day 1A call from PlantiqX to understand the plant and book the demo
  3. Day 3A 60–90 second clip of the module that matches their problem
  4. Day 7A case story or the demo film
  5. Day 14One last note. After that, the monthly update only

Opted-in contacts only. Anyone who says stop is removed the same day.

Qualifying in three questions

  • Is it a manufacturing or engineering plant, or a shipper or transporter for FreightBid?
  • Does this person own the problem, or sit next to whoever does?
  • Is there a live problem and a rough timeline? Hot, warm or nurture.

Tracking

UTM tags on every link. LinkedIn Insight Tag and Meta Pixel on the website. GA4 conversions for form submits, WhatsApp clicks and call clicks. One shared lead sheet (or HubSpot’s free CRM) with source, persona, module, stage and next action. A 15-minute lead review with PlantiqX every Monday.
