Five takeaways for the pitch deck

  1. The pain is already measured: 62% of equipment-heavy firms keep machine data across several systems, and about 59% lack structured maintenance histories.MIT SMR India x Infinite Uptime, Mar 2026

  3. Every competitor we verified sells one module, and the PdM and vision leaders chase steel, cement and fertiliser majors. Mid-size, multi-module is open ground.Infinite Uptime, Ripik.ai, Veris, SuperProcure sources below
  4. Big money validates each category: Infinite Uptime $35M (2025), Tata Motors Rs 270 cr+ into Freight Tiger (2023 to 2025), Schneider bought Zenatix (2023), Siemens bought Senseye (2022).TechCrunch, Autocar Pro, MergerLinks, Siemens

02The gap PlantiqX can own

Competitors split the plant into single jobs, and the strongest of them sell to the largest plants. Nobody we found joins the gate, the machines, the cameras, the meters and the freight desk for a mid-size plant.

Placement is Busy Fox's reading of each player's own site and customer list (inferred), not measured data.

The line to own

The gate, the machines, the cameras, the meters and the freight desk of a mid-size plant on one login, retrofitted onto the machines it already has, with a team that builds the sensor or panel when nothing off the shelf fits.

Why it is credible: a competitor's own research (MIT SMR India x Infinite Uptime, 2026) finds 62% of firms keep machine and process data scattered across systems. That is the one-platform argument.

Closest threats (inferred): Faclon Labs (energy, machines and utilities in one) and Altizon (platform story). Elmeasure looks more like a partner, since its meters are already on panels PlantiqX would read over Modbus.

Still to prove: uptime gained, rupees saved on energy and freight, and gate time saved, from the two live VEMS clients and the furnace project. Until then these are claims, not proof.
