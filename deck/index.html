Your plant already makes the data. It just lives in six places.

Nobody sees the whole plant in one place, so decisions run on memory, phone calls and month-end bills.

  • MachinesReadings stay locked inside the controllers
  • MaintenanceHistory lives in spreadsheets and people’s memory
  • The gatePeople, vehicles and material go into a paper register
  • EnergyMeters are read by hand; one bill arrives at month end
  • CamerasFootage gets watched after something has gone wrong
  • FreightRates are agreed over phone calls and follow-ups
